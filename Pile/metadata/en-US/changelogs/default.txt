- Favorites: star a document and find it in the new Favorites screen.
- Document lists now show real thumbnails of your files instead of a generic icon.
- Recognize Text: on-device OCR. The extracted text is selectable and editable.
- Per document PIN lock. The global App Lock has been removed.
- Local Backup & Restore: export your data to a file on your device and restore it later. Cloud Backup has been removed.
- About & Support now shows the support QR code, Instagram, GitHub and email.
- Recycle Bin: deleted documents are kept for 30 days and can be restored before they are permanently deleted.
- Export options: export any document as a PDF file or as JPG/PNG images (one file per page).
